Project overview

Improved key user journeys ahead of Black Friday across three white-label mobile apps for Via, a major marketplace company, reducing bounce rate and increasing product discoverability and accessibility.

Easier Login Journey

The login process was a major challenge, with high bounce rates disrupting key user flows, particularly during the purchasing journey.

To address this, we optimized the biometric login page, streamlining the experience to minimize friction. These improvements significantly reduced bounce rates, positively impacting the buying journey and reflected in increased sales.

Finding Easier What You're Looking For

We enhanced navigation by adding quick-access cards to the homepage, spotlighting popular categories like mobile devices and electronics.

This made it easier for users to locate key items, ultimately boosting interaction and improving user engagement during peak sales.

Reviewing App UI for High Usage

Ahead of Black Friday, the app's UI underwent updates to align with the new design system guidelines.

These included enhancements to text styles, color systems, and formatting, improving accessibility and delivering a more inclusive user experience.

Learnings & Reflections

This project demonstrated the critical importance of optimizing key user journeys before high-traffic periods. Small improvements in login friction and navigation can have significant impacts on conversion rates and sales.

Working within Via's UX Design Tribe highlighted the value of collaborative design critiques and user research in identifying pain points quickly and implementing effective solutions under tight deadlines.

The emphasis on accessibility and design system compliance not only improved the user experience but also created a more maintainable and scalable product for future updates.
